## Los Angeles Has Hard Water

The Los Angeles Department of Water and Power explains that water hardness varies across the city because Los Angeles receives water from multiple sources.

LADWP classifies water above seven grains per gallon as hard.

Its published figures show the San Fernando Valley and western Los Angeles averaging approximately 8.6 grains per gallon, while some other Los Angeles water-quality areas have historically recorded even higher averages.

Hard water is not the same thing as unsafe water.

It simply contains dissolved minerals, particularly calcium and magnesium compounds.

When a water droplet evaporates, the water disappears.

The minerals do not.

They remain on the glass.

## Where Do the Spots Come From?

Common sources include:

Sprinklers hitting exterior windows.

Hose water drying on glass.

Rain mixed with dirt and existing mineral residue.

Pool splash.

Irrigation overspray.

Shower glass.

Improperly dried window cleaning.

A sprinkler aimed slightly too high can repeatedly coat the same window every morning.

The homeowner cleans the glass.

The sprinkler puts the minerals back.

The cycle continues.

## Hard-Water Spots Are Not Ordinary Dirt

This is the most important distinction.

Ordinary dirt sits on the surface and can often be removed using standard window-cleaning methods.

Mineral deposits adhere more strongly.

As buildup becomes heavier, ordinary glass cleaner may remove fingerprints and dust while leaving the mineral pattern untouched.

That is why the window can be technically “clean” and still look terrible in direct sunlight.

## Why Vinegar Sometimes Appears to Work

Mild acidic solutions can interact with certain mineral deposits.

That is why vinegar appears in countless household tips.

However, not every glass installation consists of bare glass.

Windows may contain:

Special coatings.

Tint films.

Aftermarket treatments.

Painted frames.

Natural-stone surrounds.

Metal components.

Using the wrong chemical can affect adjacent materials or specialty coatings.

Always follow manufacturer guidance.

For substantial buildup, professional evaluation is safer than progressively trying stronger DIY mixtures.

## Why Scrapers Require Caution

A razor blade looks like an obvious solution for stubborn material.

But improper scraping can scratch glass or damage coatings.

Certain types of glass also require specific handling.

Professionals evaluate the surface before choosing an abrasive or scraping method.

The objective is not merely to remove the spot.

It is to remove it **without creating permanent damage**.

## Why Microfiber Technique Matters

Some windows look cloudy because cleaner residue remains behind.

## Can Hard-Water Stains Become Permanent?

Mineral buildup should not be ignored indefinitely.

Long-term deposits can become progressively harder to remove and, in some circumstances, glass can develop surface damage or etching that cleaning alone cannot reverse.

That is why prevention is significantly easier than restoration.

## How to Prevent Hard-Water Stains

### Redirect Sprinklers

This may be the single most effective step.

Water used for landscaping should not repeatedly strike windows.

Adjust sprinkler heads and replace malfunctioning nozzles.

### Dry Windows After Hose Exposure

Do not leave hard water to evaporate if it can reasonably be removed.

### Maintain Glass Regularly

Light deposits are generally easier to address than years of accumulation.

### Inspect Irrigation

Many homeowners focus on the glass while ignoring the machine depositing minerals onto it every morning.

Fix the source.

### Pay Attention to Shower Glass

The same principle applies indoors.

Use a squeegee after showering where practical.

Reducing the amount of water allowed to evaporate on glass reduces deposits.

## What If the Cloudiness Is Between the Panes?

Cleaning cannot reach the sealed space between panes of insulated glass.

If fogging appears to exist inside a double-pane window rather than on an accessible surface, the problem may involve the window unit rather than ordinary cleaning.

No amount of exterior polishing will remove something trapped inside the sealed assembly.

That is another reason professional inspection can be useful.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do my windows still look dirty after I clean them?
Often the film is mineral deposit from hard water, not ordinary dirt. Standard glass cleaner removes fingerprints and dust and leaves the mineral pattern behind.

### Is Los Angeles water actually hard?
Yes in much of the city. LADWP classifies water above seven grains per gallon as hard. Published figures for the San Fernando Valley and western Los Angeles have averaged about 8.6 grains per gallon.

### Can hard-water stains become permanent?
Ignored long enough, deposits get harder to remove, and glass can etch. Cleaning cannot reverse etching. Prevention — especially redirecting sprinklers — is easier than restoration.

### Is fog between double-pane glass a cleaning problem?
No. The sealed space between panes is not accessible. If the cloud is inside the unit, the window itself may have failed.
